Community Involvement

We believe a local bookstore should be more than a place to buy books. We are proud to support readers, families, schools, and organizations throughout our community.

Supporting Local Schools

We partner with local schools to encourage reading and help students discover books they love. Our bookstore supports book drives, reading programs, and classroom libraries.

Local Author Support

We provide local writers with opportunities to share their work through book signings, author events, and featured displays throughout the store.

Book Donations

We collect new and gently used books to donate to local organizations, schools, and families to help make reading more accessible throughout our community.

Community Events

From children's story time to book clubs and special events, we create opportunities for readers of all ages to meet, connect, and celebrate their love of books.
